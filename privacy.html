Privacy policy

Email Assistant is a private tool used only by the members of one household for their own Google accounts.

What data is accessed

With the account owner's consent, the app reads emails and attachments in that owner's Gmail account (read-only), sends summary emails from the owner's account to the owner's household, and stores documents and summaries in the owner's own Google Drive.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and stored there and in the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions: use the support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
